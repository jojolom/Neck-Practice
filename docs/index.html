Privacy Policy

Guitar Man does not collect or share personal data. The app runs fully on device, and anything it keeps stays on your device.

  • No analytics or tracking SDKs are used.
  • No account creation or sign-in is required.
  • No personal data is collected or sold.
  • Microphone: used on your device for the tuner and the looper. Audio is never uploaded or shared.
  • Saved loop layers: if you choose to save a looper layer, it is stored only on your device. It is removed when you delete it in the app or delete the app.
  • Practice reminders: scheduled as local notifications on your device. No server is involved.
  • Screen Time: if you turn on “Block apps until I practice,” the apps you choose are covered using Apple’s Screen Time features. Your selection stays on your device, is not visible to the app as app names, and is never collected or shared.

If you contact support via email, the email address and message content are used only to respond to your request. Support emails are not shared with third parties.

Children’s privacy: Guitar Man does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Policy updates: If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
